The programme

Four habits, in the order that works.

See all twelve weeks

10 to 20 minutes a day

You do not need to touch your toes.

Beginner yoga and mobility filmed from the front and the side, so you can actually see what the shape is. Every pose has an easier version, and we show that one first.

  • 10 min Day one, no experienceThe eight shapes the rest of the programme is built on.
  • 15 min Desk back and stiff hipsFor anyone who sits for a living and feels it by Wednesday.
  • 20 min Strength without a gymBodyweight work you can do in a hallway, no kit at all.

One change at a time

No diets. No food you have to pretend to like.

We do not count calories or ban anything. You add one habit a fortnight, keep the food you already cook, and let the changes stack up over a few months.

  • Week 1 Protein at breakfastThe single change most people feel within a week.
  • Week 3 Build a plate, not a recipeA method for dinner that survives a bad day.
  • Week 5 Drinking, honestlyWhat alcohol does to sleep and training, without the lecture.

The hour before bed

The hour before bed decides the next day.

Short wind-down routines and the boring practical things that move the needle: light, temperature, caffeine timing, and what to do at 3am instead of checking your phone.

  • 12 min Wind-down sequenceSlow shapes and breathing you can do on the bed.
  • Guide Your caffeine cut-offWorking out the time that actually suits you.
  • 8 min Awake at 3amA breathing pattern for the night you cannot switch off.

2 to 10 minutes

For the tight chest at three on a Tuesday.

Breathing is the fastest lever you have and the one nobody teaches you. These are short, unfussy practices you can use at a desk, in a car, or before something you are dreading.

  • 2 min The resetTwo minutes to drop your shoulders and slow your pulse.
  • 6 min Before something hardFor the meeting, the scan, the difficult call.
  • 10 min Sitting still, first attemptMeditation for people who find meditation annoying.

What to expect

What changes, and roughly when.

The full twelve weeks
Week 2

You stop negotiating with yourself

Ten minutes is too short to argue about. The habit tends to stick here, well before anything visible happens.

Week 4

Sleep moves first

The caffeine cut-off and the wind-down hour usually land before anything else. Most people notice their mornings before they notice their body.

Week 8

Stiffness stops being the default

Getting off the floor, reaching a top shelf, sitting through a long meeting. The small things quietly stop costing effort.

Week 12

You keep three things

Nobody keeps twelve habits. You keep the handful that fit your actual life and drop the rest without guilt.

Weeks 1 to 4

Foundations

One habit at a time and nothing more. Ten minute sessions.

Weeks 5 to 8

Building

Sessions get longer. Food and sleep stop being theoretical.

Weeks 9 to 12

Keeping

Strip back to the habits that survive a bad week.

How it works

Keep what works

Who this is for

Not a women's class with a men's version bolted on.

Built for beginners, not for photos

Nobody in our videos is doing a handstand. The people demonstrating are the same age and the same stiffness as the people watching.

Men and women, same programme

The habits that make you sleep better and move without aching are not gendered. Roughly half our members are men, most of whom had never done yoga before.

No weigh-ins, no before and after

We do not ask for your weight and we do not photograph anyone's stomach. You measure this by how you sleep and how you feel on the stairs.

Before you begin. Softstart gives general movement, food and sleep guidance. It is not medical advice and it does not replace your doctor.
